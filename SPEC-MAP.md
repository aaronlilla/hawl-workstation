# Hawl Workstation: SPEC-MAP

Every SPEC line S4 to S50 from the m2 contract (`E:\hawl-platform\_work\2026-10-18-scenarios\m2-contract.md`, section 1), with the workstation screen that shows it, where the data comes from, and how it will be proven in the running app. Status is `todo` for all 47 lines (2026-10-19): nothing is built.

How to read it:
- Screen names are planned components under `src/hawl/renderer/` (see `ARCHITECTURE.md`). Names are proposals until built.
- Data source: relay route or tool from the contract (section 3 of the contract). Contract routes are not shipped on hawl-platform origin/main as of 2026-10-19 (plan), so every row depends on the m2 builders landing the matching relay piece. "(web)" marks a row where the web app already covers part of it and the workstation reuses the same route.
- Proof: what a person does in the running app and what must be on screen. Each proof is run in the real app with a screenshot, against a local or staging relay, and is not "close enough". Where a proof needs a second yard, a second site or a phone, it says so.
- Rows marked "relay only" are enforced by the relay; the workstation proves it by showing the refusal or result, and the relay test (T1 in the contract) proves the rule itself.

Short names used below: Sidebar tree = site switcher + yard + stations + agents. Pane chip = chip and strip in the terminal pane header. Job drawer = per-job panel with hops and receipt. Yard console = menu, foreman, ledger, team check, doctor. Needs tray = approvals and questions in place.
